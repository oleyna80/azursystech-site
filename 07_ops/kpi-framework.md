# KPI Framework — AzurSysTech

## Acquisition

- Website visits / week
- Facebook page reach
- Google Business Profile views
- Inbound contact form submissions

## Conversion

- Leads → first response rate
- Leads → quote sent rate
- Quotes → accepted (close rate)

## Response Speed

- Avg. time to first reply (target: < 1 hour)
- Avg. time to quote delivery (target: < 24 hours)

## Reviews

- Total Google reviews
- Average rating (target: ≥ 4.5 ★)
- Reviews collected / month

## Retention

- Repeat clients / quarter
- Referral leads / month
- Client satisfaction score
