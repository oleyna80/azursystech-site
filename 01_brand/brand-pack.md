## 1. Brand decision

### Selected working brand
**AzurSysTech**

### Brand rationale
The brand combines:
- **Azur** → local Mediterranean / Côte d’Azur association
- **Sys** → systems / infrastructure / structured IT capability
- **Tech** → clear technical service signal

### Why this name works
- sounds neutral and international
- readable in French
- compatible with local positioning
- broad enough for both home users and TPE
- broader than simple “PC repair”
- can grow later into more structured IT services

---

## 2. Brand positioning

### Positioning style
**Informatique de proximité**

### Market perception goal
The brand should feel:
- local
- practical
- trustworthy
- clear
- competent
- accessible

### At launch, the brand should NOT feel like
- a big corporate IT integrator
- an abstract startup
- a pure AI company
- a low-end repair kiosk
- a brand only for advanced enterprise infrastructure

## 6. Tone of voice

### Voice principles
- simple
- direct
- reassuring
- practical
- local
- human
- structured

### Communication rules
- speak in client outcomes, not in internal tech jargon
- make services understandable in under 10 seconds
- sound reliable, not flashy
- use short, clear sentences
- emphasize intervention, configuration, dépannage, installation

### Avoid
- buzzwords
- startup language
- AI-heavy language
- overpromising
- highly technical front-page language

## 10. Visual identity direction

### Visual tone
- clean
- local
- practical
- modern but simple

### Suggested palette
- warm light background
- dark slate / ink text
- restrained teal accent
- terracotta secondary accent
- calm, non-corporate contrast

### Approved MVP direction
- visual direction: `Local Professional`
- base background: `#F6F1E8`
- surface: `#FFFDFC`
- primary text: `#1F2A37`
- primary accent: `#1F6F78`
- secondary accent: `#C96F4A`
- border / muted line: `#D8D0C4`

### Typography direction
- headings: expressive but restrained serif
- body: neutral readable sans-serif
- tone: trustworthy, calm, local, practical

### Image direction
- minimal service-oriented illustrations
- workstation / Wi-Fi / printer / laptop icons
- local and practical vibe through color and layout, not stock-photo clutter
- no cyberpunk / hacker aesthetics
- no noisy generic startup gradients
- no cold corporate look
