## 2. Main positioning

### Positioning statement
Support informatique pratique pour particuliers et petites entreprises à Nice et alentours : dépannage, installation, réseau local, Wi-Fi, imprimantes et mise en service de postes de travail.

### Expanded positioning
J’aide les particuliers et les petites entreprises à résoudre rapidement leurs problèmes informatiques et à mettre en place un environnement simple, stable et fonctionnel : ordinateurs, Wi-Fi, réseau local, imprimantes, dossiers partagés, nouveaux postes de travail.

### Russian interpretation
Практичная IT-помощь для частных клиентов и малого бизнеса в Ницце и окрестностях: ремонт, установка, настройка локальной сети, Wi-Fi, принтеров и рабочих станций.

## 3. Business model hypothesis

### Why clients should buy
Clients do not buy technologies.
They buy outcomes:

- internet works
- Wi-Fi becomes stable
- printer works
- new PC is ready to use
- files are shared correctly
- office setup works without chaos
- one specialist can handle the whole small setup

### Main value proposition
One local specialist who can:
- diagnose clearly
- explain simply
- solve practical IT problems
- help both at home and in small business environments

### Key trust advantage
The offer is stronger than a typical “PC repair only” service because it combines:
- dépannage
- réseau / Wi-Fi
- installation
- small business IT setup
- practical systems thinking

## 4. Target segments

# Segment A — Particuliers

## Typical needs
- PC lent / bug / ne démarre pas
- nouveau PC à installer
- migration ancien → nouveau PC
- Wi-Fi instable
- imprimante ne fonctionne pas
- partage de fichiers à la maison
- optimisation / upgrade ordinateur
- installation Windows / logiciels
- sauvegarde simple

## Buying triggers
- urgent need
- frustration
- no technical knowledge
- wants one person to solve everything
- wants simple explanation
- prefers local specialist

## Main expectations
- quick response
- clear pricing
- simple language
- practical result
- trust and reassurance

## What to avoid in messaging
- too much jargon
- vague service descriptions
- overly technical language
- “enterprise tone” for home users

---

# Segment B — TPE / petits commerces / indépendants

## Typical needs
- installation de postes de travail
- Wi-Fi / réseau local
- imprimantes réseau
- dossiers partagés
- configuration de base du bureau
- ajout de nouveaux postes
- maintenance simple
- migration petit matériel
- installation sur site
- support ponctuel

## Buying triggers
- opening a small office/shop
- adding employees/workstations
- unstable current setup
- no in-house IT
- wants one external specialist
- needs practical and cost-conscious support

## Main expectations
- reliability
- practical deployment
- responsiveness
- structured intervention
- ability to handle multiple devices
- ability to explain and document simply

## What to avoid in messaging
- “big enterprise” tone
- too much focus on advanced cloud
- looking too expensive or too abstract
- looking like “PC repair only”

2. installation nouveau PC
3. configuration Wi-Fi
4. configuration imprimante
5. installation logiciels
6. optimisation / upgrade PC

### MVP services for TPE
1. installation postes de travail
2. configuration réseau local
3. configuration Wi-Fi
4. imprimantes réseau
5. dossiers partagés
6. petite maintenance informatique
7. intervention sur site

### Services not pushed in MVP
- advanced cloud consulting
- heavy database architecture
- advanced server engineering
- cybersecurity audits

## 9. Pricing positioning logic

### Strategic pricing principle
At launch, pricing must:
- not look cheap
- not look enterprise-expensive
- feel fair and understandable
- support trust

### Initial recommended positioning
- particuliers: accessible and clear
- TPE: structured and slightly higher
- on-site intervention: minimum intervention logic
- packaged services preferred when possible

### Commercial angle
Sell clarity and result first.
Do not lead with hourly pricing alone.

## 13. Brand direction

Brand should feel:
- local
- trustworthy
- practical
- simple
- technical but accessible

### Avoid
- overly geeky naming
- too abstract AI-style naming
- cold corporate branding
- naming that limits service scope too much

### Good naming directions
- city + informatique
- support + local area
- practical tech service naming
- personal-brand style if needed

### Naming criteria
- easy to read in French
- easy to remember
- domain likely available
- suitable for both particuliers and TPE
- not too narrow
- can grow later
