## 1. Offer strategy

### Strategic objective
Build a simple service offer that:
- is easy to understand
- converts fast
- works for both particuliers and TPE
- supports local acquisition
- does not overwhelm the visitor

### Commercial rule
At launch, do not sell “all technical capabilities”.
Sell:
- clear service categories
- understandable outcomes
- local intervention
- practical results
- transparent entry pricing

### Pricing presentation rule
Use:
- **prix à partir de**
- **devis selon besoin**
- **diagnostic / intervention sur place**
- no heavy technical pricing grid

---

## 2. Service structure

We will organize offers into 3 levels:

### Level 1 — Fast conversion offers
Simple services that people understand immediately and can request quickly.

### Level 2 — Higher-value practical setup offers
Services that solve a wider environment problem.

### Level 3 — Business setup offers
Services for TPE / small offices / shops.

## 9. Pricing framework

### Pricing principles
- clear entry point
- no “too cheap” signal
- professional but accessible
- suitable for Nice + 30 km local service

### Recommended launch presentation
- Dépannage à domicile: **à partir de 50 €**
- Intervention professionnelle: **à partir de 65 €**
- Installation PC: **à partir de 80 €**
- Réseau / Wi-Fi / imprimante: **à partir de 70–120 €**
- TPE setup: **sur devis**

### Travel logic
Optional commercial note:
- déplacement inclus sur Nice
- hors Nice selon distance
- or
- intervention dans un rayon de 30 km

This should stay simple and not create friction.

## 10. What not to emphasize on homepage

Do not push as primary offers:
- server architecture
- advanced databases
- DevOps
- AI projects
- advanced automation
- enterprise-level infrastructure consulting

These can appear later:
- in “other services”
- in direct conversation
- in custom quotes

## 11. Offer framing language

### Good framing
- installation simple et efficace
- dépannage clair
- intervention sur site
- mise en service
- configuration pratique
- environnement fonctionnel
- accompagnement informatique local

### Avoid
- architecture systèmes complexes
- orchestration multi-agent
- infrastructures cloud avancées
- deep server jargon
- overly technical labels on public pages

## 14. Operational scope boundaries

To avoid chaos, launch scope should be limited.

### In scope
- onsite setup
- home users
- small businesses
- local Wi-Fi / network / printer / PC setup
- practical troubleshooting
- simple shared file environments
- workstation rollout

### Out of scope at launch
- large office projects
- enterprise server architecture
- high-availability systems
- full managed IT services
- advanced cloud migration
- large-scale cybersecurity services

## 17. Acceptance criteria

Offer stack is validated if:
- a home user can identify at least 1 relevant service in under 10 seconds
- a TPE owner sees at least 1 business-relevant offer immediately
- the business looks broader than “PC repair only”
- pricing looks understandable and not intimidating
- contact CTA is obvious on every main offer
