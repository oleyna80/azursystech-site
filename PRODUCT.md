# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AzurSysTech serves small businesses, independent professionals, and TPE owners in Nice and nearby areas. The primary users are business owners who need a practical way to receive, qualify, and process incoming client requests through a website, contact form, brief, AI intake agent, and simple notifications. Private clients and local IT support requests remain secondary.

## Product Purpose

The site must explain AzurSysTech's offer clearly and convert qualified visitors into contact or brief submissions. The primary business direction is web systems and AI automation for small businesses: websites, intelligent forms, AI intake agents, lead notifications, and lightweight CRM or spreadsheet integration. Local IT support is positioned as a complementary trust-building service, not the main acquisition identity.

Success means visitors quickly understand what AzurSysTech does, see practical outcomes rather than AI buzzwords, and know whether to use the contact form or brief.

## Brand Personality

Practical, local, trustworthy.

The voice is simple, direct, reassuring, human, and structured. It should feel like a competent local specialist, not a large corporate integrator, a low-end repair kiosk, or an abstract AI startup.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like:
- a generic SaaS landing page;
- a pure AI startup with vague automation promises;
- an enterprise consulting firm;
- a PC repair-only service;
- a flashy template built around buzzwords, oversized gradients, or decorative cards.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the current positioning: web systems and AI automation for small businesses in Nice.
2. Keep IT support visible as a secondary service and trust entry point.
3. Explain outcomes in plain language: receive requests, qualify leads, notify the owner, prepare follow-up.
4. Make the conversion path obvious: contact form for quick requests, brief for automation or website projects.
5. Preserve local trust through clear geography, practical scope, and restrained visual confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for practical WCAG AA behavior: readable contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, meaningful link text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives for any non-essential animation. The site must remain clear in French and Russian.
