# Implementation brief — automatiser-demandes-clients guide

## Audience and job

Owner of a small company evaluating a first repetitive client-request workflow. The page should answer the practical “where do I start?” question before presenting the existing brief flow.

## Composition

- Exact localized H1 and a short answer block near the top.
- Clear progression from current-state mapping to deterministic workflow, AI boundary, human control, privacy, checklist, and FAQ.
- One generic artisan/local-service scenario, explicitly not a client case study.
- Localized inline links to the existing Nice service, AI automation explanation, portfolio/examples, and brief.

## Design constraints

Use existing site primitives, spacing, colors, typography, icons, and responsive behavior. Keep the guide editorial and scannable; do not create a blog/resources index, card-heavy redesign, new animation system, or new image asset.

## Content constraints

All FR/RU/EN copy is authored in the locale data source. Structured data reuses that source. Avoid statistics, outcomes, testimonials, guarantees, deadlines, addresses, and sensitive-data collection claims.
